# Code of Conduct

We want project spaces to be welcoming, constructive, and safe. Treat people with respect, assume good intent while addressing impact, welcome questions, and critique ideas rather than individuals.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's private content are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a non-public GitHub contact channel. Do not include lyric content, credentials, or other personal data unless strictly necessary. Maintainers will review reports in good faith, preserve confidentiality where practical, and avoid conflicts of interest.

This is a project-specific policy and is not a claim of affiliation with another community standard.
